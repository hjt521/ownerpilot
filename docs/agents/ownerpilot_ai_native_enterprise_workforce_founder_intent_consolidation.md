## 1. Executive Summary

OwnerPilot already has a ratified enterprise architecture, a ratified cognitive architecture, a ratified closed-loop learning architecture, and a small implemented executive-agent foundation. It does not yet have a fully recovered or ratified enterprise AI workforce architecture.

The source recovery confirms five distinct layers that must not be collapsed:

1. Human constitutional authority and Founder ratification
2. Independent governance, review, audit, and dissent
3. Executive advisory roles and implementation-level operating postures
4. Cognitive architecture, reasoning engines, operating-system concepts, and evaluation programs
5. State-changing implementation and deployment operators

The Founder has now clarified an intended future operating model:

> Automation and AI agents should become the functioning backbone of OwnerPilot. Human work and human decision-making should be minimized. The Founder should not participate in daily operations, but should re-enter when introducing new ideas, marketing concepts, products, strategic direction, or other material Founder initiatives. OwnerPilot should continuously maintain and improve the “Winning Strategy” in the background.

This is an important new Founder direction. It is not yet recovered canon and should not be silently treated as already ratified architecture. It should be appended to the recovery package as a proposed Founder Intent and Target Operating Model for Architect reconciliation.

## 11. Core Conflicts and Reconciliation Issues

### 11.1 Capability versus role

EA-100 capabilities do not equal departments or executives.

A future workforce architecture may map capabilities to departments and agents, but that mapping requires explicit design and Founder ratification.

### 11.2 Engine versus employee

Reasoning engines are system components, not employees.

A future CFO Agent might consume Financial Intelligence Engine outputs, but the engine itself should not be silently renamed into an executive.

### 11.3 Historical executive-title overlap

CTO, Chief Systems Architect, Chief Product Builder, Constitutional Implementation Officer, Chief Configuration Manager, Constitutional Librarian, and Release Manager overlap.

The Architect should normalize them into a small number of clear roles rather than preserve title proliferation.

### 11.4 Review board versus Board of Directors

Independent Architecture Review Board is verified.

AI Board of Directors is not yet verified as a ratified corporate body.

### 11.5 OPOS meaning

OPOS is currently the reasoning kernel.

Using OPOS to also mean a full autonomous enterprise operating system would create architectural ambiguity unless explicitly reconciled.

### 11.6 Autonomy versus constitutional control

The Founder’s new automation-first intent requires extensive autonomy, but current constitutional rules preserve:

- human review for material adverse action;
- no AI self-ratification;
- no silent authority expansion;
- no uncontrolled Production writes;
- no hidden learning or silent policy change.

The target architecture must minimize human involvement without eliminating constitutional control.

# PART II — NEW FOUNDER INTENT FOR ARCHITECT RECONCILIATION

## 12. Founder Target Operating Model

The Founder’s intended future OwnerPilot company is an AI-native enterprise in which automation and agents perform the overwhelming majority of recurring work.

### 12.1 Founder’s desired role

The Founder should not be required for daily operations.

The Founder should primarily participate when:

- introducing new product ideas;
- introducing marketing ideas;
- setting or changing strategic direction;
- defining new customer segments;
- proposing new business models;
- approving major capital, legal, reputational, or irreversible decisions;
- changing OwnerPilot’s constitutional purpose or risk posture.

The Founder should not be routinely required for:

- ordinary task routing;
- routine prioritization;
- routine research;
- routine implementation;
- routine QA;
- ordinary deployment to permitted environments;
- recurring reporting;
- ordinary customer support;
- ordinary marketing execution;
- routine pricing analysis;
- routine documentation;
- standard operational monitoring.

### 12.2 Automation-first principle

The target system should follow:

> Automate by default. Escalate only by exception.

Human involvement should be reserved for decisions that are:

- constitutionally reserved;
- materially irreversible;
- legally or regulatorily sensitive;
- financially material beyond approved thresholds;
- outside ratified policy;
- low-confidence with material consequence;
- strategically novel;
- in conflict with Founder intent;
- blocked by unresolved dissent.

### 12.3 Founder as strategic originator, not daily operator

The Founder should function as:

- source of vision;
- source of new product ideas;
- source of new marketing concepts;
- final ratifier of material strategic changes;
- constitutional sovereign;
- exception-level escalation point.

The Founder should not function as the everyday queue manager for AI agents.

## 13. Winning Strategy as a Continuous Background Capability

“Winning Strategy” appears in RP-005 and RP-006 as part of the Executive Decision Intelligence vision.

The Founder now wants the Winning Strategy to operate continuously in the background.

### 13.1 Proposed normalized function

A future Winning Strategy Function should continuously:

- evaluate OwnerPilot’s current objectives;
- monitor customer, product, market, operational, financial, and regulatory evidence;
- compare available strategies;
- identify changes in assumptions;
- detect emerging opportunities and threats;
- preserve optionality;
- recommend strategic adjustments;
- test recommendations against alternatives;
- monitor whether prior decisions are producing intended outcomes;
- trigger review when evidence materially changes.

### 13.2 Required controls

The Winning Strategy Function should not:

- become a single composite score;
- silently override critical failures;
- autonomously ratify strategy;
- execute irreversible actions outside approved policy;
- suppress dissent;
- bypass legal, privacy, security, or constitutional controls;
- optimize only for revenue at the expense of trust, legality, or long-term enterprise value.

It should remain:

- multidimensional;
- qualitative-first for decision control;
- evidence-based;
- uncertainty-aware;
- explainable;
- continuously evaluated;
- subject to independent dissent;
- bounded by ratified objectives and thresholds.

### 13.3 Background operating loop

```text
Observe
→ Update facts and evidence
→ Identify objective changes
→ Generate strategic alternatives
→ Evaluate financial, legal, operational, customer, market, and reputational effects
→ Preserve optionality
→ Select a proposed Winning Strategy
→ Test through dissent and simulation
→ Execute only within approved authority
→ Monitor outcomes
→ Learn
→ Reassess
```

### 13.4 Founder notification posture

The Founder should receive:

- concise strategic exception alerts;
- major opportunity briefs;
- material-risk alerts;
- new-product proposals;
- marketing-strategy proposals;
- decisions requiring ratification;
- periodic executive summaries.

The Founder should not receive every intermediate analysis, routine task, or low-level operational event.

## 15. Proposed Autonomy Model

### Level 0 — Analysis only

- no writes;
- no external communications;
- no state changes.

### Level 1 — Drafting and recommendation

- creates plans, drafts, and proposals;
- human or authorized agent approves execution.

### Level 2 — Bounded reversible execution

- may perform approved low-risk, reversible actions;
- must log all actions;
- must stay within explicit scope and thresholds.

### Level 3 — Departmental autonomous operation

- may execute recurring departmental work under ratified policy;
- exception-based escalation;
- independent assurance and rollback required.

### Level 4 — Cross-functional enterprise orchestration

- may coordinate multiple departments and operators;
- Winning Strategy runs continuously;
- material exceptions go to Founder or designated authority.

### Level 5 — Reserved

No fully sovereign AI enterprise authority should be assumed.

OwnerPilot’s Constitution, Founder authority, legal requirements, and human-only responsibilities remain controlling.

## 16. Human-Minimization Without Governance Collapse

The target is not “human removed.”

The target is:

> Human judgment concentrated at the highest-value and legally required points, with routine work delegated to governed AI systems.

The architecture should minimize Founder involvement by:

- defining policy and thresholds in advance;
- using default operating rules;
- delegating routine approvals;
- escalating by exception;
- providing concise summaries rather than raw activity;
- preserving reversible execution;
- using independent assurance agents;
- logging decisions and actions;
- maintaining rollback;
- separating advisory agents from state-changing operators;
- separating implementation from audit;
- preserving constitutional ratification for material changes.

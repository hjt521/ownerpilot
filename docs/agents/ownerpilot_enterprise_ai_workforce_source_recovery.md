## 4. Conflicts and later controls

1. **Board terminology conflict:** “Independent Review Board Challenge” is a ratified pipeline stage; it is not evidence that a standing “AI Board of Directors” was created.
2. **Executive-title conflict:** CTO/Chief Systems Architect is a narrow implementation-judgment posture in DOC-003, not a separate registered executive agent.
3. **Release-title conflict:** DOC-003 calls the repository assistant “Release Manager,” while later operational practice and agent designs prohibit autonomous merge/deploy/Production action. The later bounded authority controls the action.
4. **Capability-owner conflict:** Product and Engineering labels in EA-100 are capability owners, not proof of AI department heads.
5. **Research-to-role conflict:** OPOS, OPIL, DIRP, EDIC, Recommendation Object, Decision Graph, and Evaluation Lab are systems/programs, not personnel roles.
6. **Founder recollection limit:** the broader company-like structure is preserved as a prior concept, but missing titles, charters, dates, and approvals remain unresolved.

## RQS – OCM-001 reconciliation (RATIFIED 2026-07-25 · ADR-015 · RPT-014)
- **Status:** **Ratified.** The RQS/OCM-001 numeric-scoring conflict — flagged in RPT-012 §2 (RP-005's Outcome Projection Engine) and restated in RPT-013 §1 (RP-006's RQS) — was raised twice without resolution.
